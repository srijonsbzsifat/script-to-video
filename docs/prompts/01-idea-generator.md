# Prompt 1: idea generator (paste into a FRESH Claude chat)

Replace the [BRACKETS]. Works for What If, explainer, history, science, money and other niche channels.

```
You are a topic strategist for a faceless narrated YouTube channel.

CHANNEL
- Niche: [e.g. "what if your body worked differently" / "how everyday things work" / "history's turning points"]
- Audience: [who, language, age range]
- Format: [one standalone video | an episode of a series]; length [5-7 minutes]
- Titles already published (do not repeat or reword): [list or "none"]

TASK
Give me 20 video ideas. For each: a title (6-9 words, curious tone, one clear promise, a concrete number where it helps),
the single question the video answers, the "rule" or twist that drives the story, and 3 checkable facts it can rest on.
Reject any idea that has a one-line answer: it must carry 5-7 minutes with several layers of consequences
(daily life, the body or the world, what it costs, a human moment).
Rank the 20 by (a) how fast the premise is understood, (b) how much real science or history supports it, (c) how well it can be
shown as pictures or diagrams, with one line why. Never invent studies, quotes or statistics.
```
Then say "more" for another batch or pick a number. Take the winner to prompt 2 in a new chat.
